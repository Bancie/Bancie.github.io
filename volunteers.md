<img class="entry-media" src="{{ '/assets/images/arxiv_logo.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="arXiv" />

*4/2026 – 7/2026*

Open-source contribution to an MCP server that enables AI assistants to search and analyze arXiv papers through a simple Model Context Protocol interface.

- Fixed truncated arXiv PDF downloads by streaming `pdf_url` with httpx (merged [PR #99](https://github.com/blazickjp/arxiv-mcp-server/pull/99); related [issue #98](https://github.com/blazickjp/arxiv-mcp-server/issues/98)).
- Ranked top 2 human contributor (excluding bots) over Apr–Jul 2026.

- [MCP Market](https://mcpmarket.com/server/arxiv-1)
